Step I

Upload your data to start the forecasting analysis.

Your file should have a CSV format with two columns: 'ds' (date) and 'y' (metric). The 'ds' column should have one of "YYYY-MM-DD" or "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS" formats. The 'y' column is the metric/measure that you're interested in running the forecast for.

Step II

Enter the number of days in the future that you want to run the forecast for. Analysis takes 1-5 seconds.
